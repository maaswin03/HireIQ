🎯 Challenge Tracks
Track 1: Airia Everywhere
Meet users where they already are.

Build AI agents that seamlessly integrate into the platforms people use every day. No more context-switching, no more friction—just intelligent assistance exactly when and where it's needed.

Target Platforms:

🌐 Browser extensions
📧 Outlook & email inboxes
💬 Chat widgets
💼 Slack & Microsoft Teams
📁 SharePoint
📱 WhatsApp
🔌 Custom APIs for iterative workflows
Think: Personal AI assistants that live in your browser, email triaging bots, Slack integrations that automate team workflows, or browser extensions that enhance productivity across any web application.

Track 2: Active Agents
Orchestrate intelligence across your entire tech stack.

Design sophisticated multi-agent workflows that autonomously gather data, make decisions, and perform actions across 2+ systems. Build the autonomous workforce of tomorrow.

Key Capabilities:

🤝 Multi-system integrations
👤 Human-in-the-loop (HITL) decision points
📄 Dynamic document generation
🔄 Nested agent architectures
⚡ Automated cross-platform workflows
Think: Contract analysis pipelines, automated compliance monitoring systems, intelligent customer onboarding workflows, or research agents that synthesize information from multiple sources.

 

 

 

             
Requirements
Required Submissions
1. Agent Name
Give your AI agent a memorable name that reflects its purpose and personality. Be creative—this is how your agent will be known in the community!

2. Project Description
Provide a clear and compelling description of your agent that includes:

Problem Statement: What challenge does your agent solve?
Solution Overview: How does your agent address this problem?
Key Features: What are the main capabilities of your agent?
Technologies Used: What platforms, integrations, or Airia features power your agent?
Target Users: Who will benefit from your agent?
3. Demo Video ⭐
Create and submit a video demonstration of your agent in action.

Video Requirements:

✅ Maximum length: 4 minutes
✅ Must be hosted on YouTube or Vimeo (public or unlisted)
✅ Must show your agent working in real-time (not just slides)
✅ Should include narration or captions explaining functionality
✅ Clearly demonstrate the problem solved and value delivered
💡 Pro Tip: Keep it engaging! Show, don't just tell. Judges want to see your agent in action.

4. Airia Community Submission 🌐
Your agent MUST be published to the Airia Community to be eligible for prizes.

This is your ticket to entry! Follow these steps:

Log into your Airia account
Navigate to the Community section
Click "Share Agent" or "Publish to Community"
Complete the agent listing with name, description, and relevant tags
Ensure your agent is set to Public visibility
Copy the Community URL to include in your DevPost submission

Judging Criteria
Technological Implementation
Does the project demonstrate quality software development? Does the project thoroughly leverage the required tool? How is the quality of the code?
Design
Is the user experience and design of the project well thought out? Is there a balanced blend of frontend and backend in the software?
Potential Impact
How big of an impact could the project have on the enterprise? How big of an impact could it have in the market?
Quality of Idea
How creative and unique is the project? Does the concept exist already? If so, how much does the project improve on it? *
